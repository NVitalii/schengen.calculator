Understanding the Schengen 90/180 Rule

The Schengen 90/180 rule states that you can stay in the Schengen Area for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. This is a rolling window, meaning the 180 days look backwards from any given date.

Manually calculating this using a calendar is highly prone to errors. Our Schengen calculator app ensures you never accidentally overstay and face deportation or bans.
