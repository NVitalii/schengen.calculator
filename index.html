Understanding the Schengen 90/180 Rule

The Schengen 90/180 rule states that you can stay in the Schengen Area for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. This is a rolling window, meaning the 180 days look backwards from any given date.

Manually calculating this using a calendar is highly prone to errors. Our Schengen calculator app ensures you never accidentally overstay and face deportation or bans.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 90/180 rule?

It allows non-EU citizens to stay in the Schengen Zone for a maximum of 90 days in any 180-day period.

Is the calculation accurate?

Yes, our algorithm is fully compliant with the official EU border control rules, including entry and exit day counting and DST edge cases.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once downloaded, the app stores your trips locally and requires no internet connection to calculate your days.

Are entry and exit days included?

Yes. Under EU border guidelines, any part of a day spent in the Schengen zone counts as a full day — both the day you enter and the day you exit, even if you crossed the border right before midnight.

Can I plan future trips with it?

Yes. Add planned trips and check the calendar: every future date shows exactly how many days you will have available, so you can book tickets with confidence.
