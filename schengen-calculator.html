How to Use This Calculator

1

Enter your stays

Press "Add Stay" and type the entry and exit dates of every visit to the Schengen Area in the last 180 days — and any trips you have booked ahead.

2

Read the ring

The ring shows the days left as of today; both the entry day and the exit day count as full days, exactly as EU border guards count them.

3

Check any date

Tap a date in the calendar to see the allowance on that exact day. A red number means an overstay — move the trip until every day is green.

Questions About the Online Calculator

Where are my trips stored?

Only in this browser, on this device (HTML5 local storage). Nothing is uploaded to any server, so there is no account, no sign-up and nothing to delete — clearing the browser's site data removes the trips.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Yes — the online calculator is free, with no limits and no ads. We make the mobile app with the same engine; if you like this page, the app is the same thing plus reminders, family profiles and offline mode.

How is the mobile app different?

The same 90/180 engine, plus what a browser page can't do: reminders before a trip and when days free up, profiles for the whole family, a home-screen widget, offline work with no internet at the border, encrypted phone-to-phone transfer, and optional extras like stay rules of other countries (UK, USA, Canada…) and cloud backup.

Want this to run by itself?

The free mobile app tracks the same 90/180 days automatically — with reminders, family profiles and full offline mode.
